## 🧭 About me

I'm a software engineer at **BAC**, where I build **international cross-border payment products** on top of the **SWIFT** network: systems where correctness, traceability, and reliability matter.

In parallel, I'm pursuing an **MSc in Software Engineering with a focus on Applied Artificial Intelligence**. These days I spend most of my time on:

- 🤖 **Data agents**: LLM-powered agents that query, reason over, and explain data
- 🕸️ **Multi-agent AI workflows**: orchestrating specialized agents to solve complex tasks end to end
- ⚙️ **Everyday automation**: small tools that remove friction from daily life
